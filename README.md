# High and Seek

A cozy mansion score run that fills the browser window. Bag as many hidden munchies as you dare across two floors and an attached Snack & Paws wing, then return to the starting lounge’s couch and TV to score the uneaten food you bring home. Six upstairs rooms hold a much bigger snack supply, along with party guests who chase you down to tell a very long story. Dodge them, or mash your way out of the conversation. There is no fixed checklist, and the game never reveals food quantities or clear-room status. Bagged snacks automatically refuel you only when their whole refill fits; each one eaten leaves the bag and cannot count toward your score. Distant food stays hidden until you search nearby, and every new run changes the supply. Includes a following camera, zoom and estate overview, in-world interaction prompts, a collected-haul display, collision-aware movement, click-to-walk pathfinding, two hints, a map for each floor, optional sound effects, and a locally saved best banked haul.

## Play locally

Run `npm start` from this directory, or `npm run dev:high-and-seek` from the parent workspace, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4173> in Google Chrome. No dependency installation or build step is required. You can also open `dist/index.html` directly.

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: move in screen directions.
- E or Space: put a nearby snack in your bag, use a staircase or wing doorway, call or wake Waffles, or finish and bank your run at the lounge couch. During a conversation, tap either key 12 times to escape; completing the escape costs 4 energy. Holding a key does not add taps.
- Click or tap a discovered snack: walk to it and put it in your bag.
- Click or tap the floor: walk there using a route around furniture and walls.
- H: use a hint. M: open the room map. P: pause.
- G or the wing button: walk to the ground-floor wing doorway (or the exit when inside). Press E nearby to enter or return; transitions are deliberate like the stairs. In the wing, E calls Waffles to follow or gently wakes him if he is asleep.
- T or the stairs button: walk to the current floor’s staircase. Press E at the stairs to change floors; walking there does not change floors automatically.
- R or the Return to couch button: walk back to the starting lounge on the ground floor. While upstairs, this routes only to the staircase down. Press E to go downstairs, then continue home. In the wing, R routes to its doorway; press E to return, then R walks to the couch. Press E beside the couch and TV to bank your haul.
- Hold Shift while walking or following a click route: sprint faster at a higher energy cost.
- Scroll or use +/−: zoom. V: toggle the whole-mansion overview and follow camera. The corner controls provide the same actions, plus optional browser fullscreen.
- The bag display shows only uneaten food you are carrying, grouped by type. Snacks you eat disappear from it.
- Touch devices also show a directional pad and a context action: GRAB for food, DOOR at a wing doorway, CALL or WAKE beside Waffles, STAIRS at a staircase, MASH during a conversation, or CHILL when you can bank at the couch. Tap MASH repeatedly to fill the escape meter.

Energy starts at 100 and drains with time and actual distance traveled. Picking up food puts it in your bag. You automatically eat a bagged snack only when its entire 16–29-point refill fits below the maximum of 100; eating removes that snack from the bag. For example, a 19-point snack stays bagged at 90 energy and is eaten at 81 energy to restore you to 100. Snack refills are about 20% smaller to keep stockpiling from removing return-trip pressure: soda 16, chips 19, ice cream 21, cookies and gummies 22, donut 24, nachos 26, and pizza 29. Food you eat keeps you going, while the uneaten snacks you bring back to the couch determine your final score. Longer detours and conversations burn through more of that potential score, so bring a surplus home before your stash runs dry. Below 50%, the world gradually fades greener as energy falls; eating snacks reduces the tint again. Save energy for the trip home. Running out ends the run without banking your current haul. Energy and the clock freeze while a menu is open or the tab is hidden. Sprinting trades more energy for speed.

The upstairs cinema, snack pantry, afterparty, guest suite, landing, and studio offer a richer haul. Guests notice you and give chase, marked by an exclamation point and a “Hey! Wait up!” warning. Keep moving and dodge around furniture to avoid getting caught. A catch starts a 16-second conversation that stops movement while time and idle energy drain continue. Mash E, Space, or the mobile MASH button: 12 distinct taps fill the escape meter and break you free for a flat 4 energy. Incomplete tapping does not charge energy, and holding a key does not fill the meter. You can also wait for the story to end. Either way, you get 6 seconds of breathing room before another conversation can begin. The staircase is a safe zone: guests stop chasing and cannot start conversations there, so you can always head downstairs.

Each guest has a distinct voice and an ongoing monologue. Max goes deep on movies, Jules has snack theories, Bea relives the afterparty, Sam rates every comfort, Leo pitches creative ideas, and Rae has another question before you go. Their dialogue advances every few seconds during a story. Meet them again and they pick up where they left off, circling back only after their whole ramble. A fresh run resets everyone's stories. Turn Sound on to hear original cartoon gibberish: each person has a distinct pitch, vocal tone and rhythm. The chatter follows each dialogue line, warbles as you mash to escape, and stops immediately when muted, paused, hidden, escaped or restarted. Voices are generated locally with Web Audio; no recordings or downloads are required.

The ground-floor doorway opens into the Snack & Paws Wing: a snack den, Waffles the corgi's playroom, and a pet kitchen with his food bowl. The clubhouse has human snacks around its seating and shelves each run. Nico patrols his den without eating until you first enter that room. After that, he competes for actual wing munchies, with crunchy bites, a voiced Mmm when sound is on, and an overhead thought bubble. His food never adds to your haul or refills your energy. His appetite resets with a fresh run. Call Waffles with E nearby and walk him to his bowl in the other room. He follows around walls and furniture, eats for two seconds, then gets full energy, a wagging tail and happy hearts. His overhead meter begins at 75 and drains 2.4 energy per active second until fed, giving you about 31 seconds from his starting 75 energy. He begs and occasionally whines when sound is on. At zero he curls up and sleeps; E nearby gently wakes him with 25 energy so you can finish the task. The bowl is already stocked and feeding him does not spend collected munchies. This task is optional. Wing actors and hunger freeze outside the wing, in menus, hidden tabs and after a run; their state persists when you leave and return. A fresh run resets everything.

Food is generated from a validated placement pool; the amount and distribution vary each run. Supplies remain hidden until you get close inside the room, or reveal a snack with one of two hints. Discovered snacks stay visible when you revisit their floor for the rest of that run. Room labels and the map show names and exploration status, never snack totals or remaining supplies.

Your run starts in the Snack Lounge, which has couches and a TV and is marked HOME. Leave the starting room and return to the marked couch area with at least one uneaten munchie still in your bag. Press E to bank those remaining snacks; neither entering the room nor collecting all available food automatically ends a run. Your final score is the number of uneaten snacks returned, so food already eaten never counts. Higher banked counts beat the record, with a faster run breaking a tie. Unbanked food never changes the record. Restart restores energy and regenerates the mansion’s supply. This bag-scoring mode saves its best record under the localStorage key `high-and-seek-best-haul-v2`, separately from earlier modes.


Banking a haul starts a nine-second ending scene before the results: the camera moves in, you sit on the lounge couch and eat the uneaten food you brought home, and the TV switches on to a bundled original Munchie TV cartoon. Portrait screens get separate close-ups of the TV and your character eating before the camera returns to the lounge. The local 10-second MP4 loops silently on the TV, so no external video service is needed. P or Pause freezes the scene; Escape or Skip scene goes to the result. The haul is saved immediately, while gameplay time and energy freeze. Hidden tabs pause the scene and video. Close the result with Enjoy the couch & TV to keep watching, or start another run. Restart stops playback and restores the normal camera and controls.
The sound control includes an original 88 BPM lo-fi funk score: warm electric piano, swung brushes and bass downstairs; an added tension layer near upstairs pursuers; quiet elevator jazz during conversations; and a warmer couch-ending groove. Four matching seamless loops crossfade on one shared audio context, with music lowered under guest voices. Music starts only after you enable sound, pauses with menus or hidden tabs, and stops on mute or page exit. On phones, enable sound through the pause menu. The local WAV files are in `dist/assets/music/`; `generate-music.py` regenerates them. Use the local server for music, since browser file-opening restrictions can block audio loading.

## Verify

Run `npm test`. `verify.mjs` checks both floors, home furniture, spawn, generated supply, guest routes and placement reachability. `qa-pathing.mjs` checks collection routes, explicit home banking, pause/resume, and 160 deterministic random starting positions. `qa-camera.mjs` checks camera transforms across five screen sizes. `qa-energy.mjs` checks energy, discovery, failure and seeded return trips. `qa-haul.mjs` covers partial scoring, return-zone and empty-haul guards, keyboard/touch interactions, best-record storage, and the absence of room count spoilers. `qa-wing.mjs` covers doorway transitions, corgi guiding and feeding, hunger/napping/waking, competing food consumption, pause and restart, and frame caps. `qa-upstairs.mjs` covers stair transitions, floor separation, guest interruptions, pausing, mash input and repeat guards, escape costs, cooldowns, restart and eight seeded upstairs collection-and-return trips. `qa-guests.mjs` checks pursuit, dodging, sightlines, furniture detours, stair safety and bounded shared navigation. `qa-monologues.mjs` checks all 120 unique lines, timed dialogue, pause, continuation, looping, restart, mash behavior and speech performance. `qa-chatter.mjs` checks distinct voices, finite audio scheduling, sound controls, speech timing and audio cleanup. `qa-munching.mjs` checks finite crunchy bites and voiced Mmm sounds, shared audio, repeated-bite limits and immediate cleanup across game transitions. `qa-music.mjs` checks original loop assets, synchronized playback, scene mixing, pause/mute, async cancellation, shared audio and frame limits. `qa-collision.mjs` checks rounded furniture corners, wall ends, swept movement and sliding. `qa-ending.mjs` checks cinematic timing, pause/skip, actual video handling, frozen scoring, selected snacks, frame caps and restart cleanup. Set `QA_FRAME_MS=1000` for path checks or `QA_SEARCH_SECONDS=8` for energy checks to exercise throttled frames and extra search time.

Mansion artwork is drawn directly in canvas. The TV uses the bundled `dist/assets/lounge-tv.mp4`; `generate-tv-video.py` can regenerate it with Pillow and Windows Media Foundation. Google Fonts is optional; system fallback fonts are provided. Only the best banked haul and its run time are stored, in browser localStorage. The hosting manifest is `.openai/hosting.json` and published static files are in `dist/`.

## Performance

Furniture and interior wall ends use rounded collision outlines. Circular players and guests slide along contact curves using the same swept movement solver, with travel charged by actual distance. Click routes validate each connecting segment against that geometry and reuse the static navigation grid for each area across restarts. Props can set `cornerRadius`; defaults are 0.22 world units for furniture and 0.07 for interior wall ends, bounded by the prop size. Exterior walls contain the player within the estate.

Active play renders at a maximum of 60 FPS, including on higher-refresh displays. The cinematic keeps the 60 FPS cap, and post-result couch/TV viewing uses 30 FPS with a 24 FPS video. The waiting screen uses 30 FPS; paused scenes redraw only when needed, and hidden tabs skip rendering. Movement, hunt time, and energy use elapsed time independently of skipped display callbacks.

Static scenery and furniture use bounded canvas caches, with at most eight million cached pixels. Camera pans reuse overscan, zooming and resizing invalidate the caches, and changing floors releases the previous floor’s textures. Guests and the avatar retain their depth ordering around furniture. HUD values update at most ten times a second and only write to the DOM when their displayed values change. Pickup, retry, and other direct actions update immediately. Pathfinding reuses typed buffers and precomputed grid coordinates. Guests share a navigation field refreshed at most four times a second while pursuing, with cached routes back to patrols; slow frames use short AI steps to preserve pursuit speed.

`qa-performance.mjs` checks the 60 FPS cap against simulated 60/120/144/240 Hz callbacks, frame jitter, pause/visibility handling, movement/energy consistency, and HUD mutation counts. `qa-render-perf.mjs` checks cache reuse, floor invalidation, painter order, fallback rendering, and memory bounds. Caching removes more than 80% of steady-frame canvas calls in the renderer harness. These are workload counts, not a hardware frame-time benchmark.
