# High and Seek

A cozy mansion score run that fills the browser window. Start in the ground-floor Snack Lounge, bag as many hidden munchies as you dare across the mansion and its wings, then reach the private TV snug directly off the Midnight Wing’s central hallway to score the uneaten food you carry. Six upstairs rooms hold a much bigger snack supply, along with party guests who chase you down to tell a very long story. Dodge them, or mash your way out of the conversation. The Midnight Wing connects to the upstairs and offers a direct hallway path to the final couch, with optional snack detours into the laundry room, walk-in wardrobe, collector’s attic, and moonlit terrace. There is no fixed checklist, and the game never reveals food quantities or clear-room status. Bagged snacks automatically refuel you only when their whole refill fits; each one eaten leaves the bag and cannot count toward your score. Distant food stays hidden until you search nearby, and every new run changes the supply. Includes a following camera, zoom and estate overview, in-world interaction prompts, a collected-haul display, collision-aware movement, click-to-walk pathfinding, two hints, a map for each area, optional sound effects, and a locally saved best banked haul.

## Play locally

Run `npm start` from this directory, or `npm run dev:high-and-seek` from the parent workspace, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4173> in Google Chrome. No dependency installation or build step is required. You can also open `dist/index.html` directly.

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: move in screen directions.
- E or Space: put a nearby snack in your bag, use a staircase or wing doorway, call, coax, or wake Waffles, use his squeaky toy, or finish and bank your run at the final TV snug couch. During a conversation, tap either key 12 times to escape; completing the escape costs 4 energy. Holding a key does not add taps.
- Click or tap a discovered snack: walk to it and put it in your bag.
- Click or tap the floor: walk there using a route around furniture and walls.
- H: use a hint. M: open the room map. P: pause.
- G or the wing button: walk to the current area’s wing doorway. The ground floor connects to Snack & Paws; the upstairs connects to the Midnight Wing. Inside either wing, G leads to its exit. Press E nearby to go through; transitions are deliberate like the stairs. In Snack & Paws, E calls Waffles to follow or gently wakes him if he is asleep.
- T or the stairs button: walk to the current area’s staircase. From the ground floor, T first leads to the Snack & Paws doorway; press E to enter and the route continues to the staircase in Waffles’s clubhouse. Press E at the stairs to go upstairs. Upstairs, T leads to the stairs back to the clubhouse. Walking to a connection never changes areas automatically.
- R or the Head to couch button: follow the route from the ground floor through the Snack & Paws doorway, up the staircase in Waffles’s clubhouse, through the upstairs Midnight Wing doorway, and along the central hallway into the TV snug. Press E at each area doorway or staircase; walking automatically continues along the next part of the route after you go through. Manual movement or clicking another destination cancels the route. The laundry, wardrobe, attic, and terrace remain optional detours. Press E beside the couch and TV to bank your haul.
- Hold Shift while walking or following a click route: sprint faster at a higher energy cost.
- Scroll or use +/−: zoom. V: toggle the whole-mansion overview and follow camera. The corner controls provide the same actions, plus optional browser fullscreen.
- The bag display shows only uneaten food you are carrying, grouped by type. Snacks you eat disappear from it.
- Touch devices also show a directional pad and a context action: GRAB for food, DOOR at a wing doorway, CALL or WAKE beside Waffles, PLAY at his squeaky toy, STAIRS at a staircase, MASH during a conversation, or CHILL when you can bank at the couch. Tap MASH repeatedly to fill the escape meter.

Energy starts at 100 and drains with time and actual distance traveled. Picking up food puts it in your bag. You automatically eat a bagged snack only when its entire 16–29-point refill fits below the maximum of 100; eating removes that snack from the bag. For example, a 19-point snack stays bagged at 90 energy and is eaten at 81 energy to restore you to 100. Snack refills: soda 16, chips 19, ice cream 21, cookies and gummies 22, donut 24, nachos 26, and pizza 29. Food you eat keeps you going, while the uneaten snacks you deliver to the couch determine your final score. Longer detours and conversations burn through more of that potential score, so bring a surplus home before your stash runs dry. Below 50%, the world gradually fades greener as energy falls; eating snacks reduces the tint again. Save energy for the journey to the final couch. Running out ends the run without banking your current haul. Energy and the clock freeze while a menu is open or the tab is hidden. Sprinting trades more energy for speed.

The upstairs cinema, snack pantry, afterparty, guest suite, landing, and studio offer a richer haul. Guests notice you and give chase, marked by an exclamation point and a “Hey! Wait up!” warning. Keep moving and dodge around furniture to avoid getting caught. A catch starts a 16-second conversation that stops movement while time and idle energy drain continue. Mash E, Space, or the mobile MASH button: 12 distinct taps fill the escape meter and break you free for a flat 4 energy. Incomplete tapping does not charge energy, and holding a key does not fill the meter. You can also wait for the story to end. Either way, you get 6 seconds of breathing room before another conversation can begin. The staircase is a safe zone: guests stop chasing and cannot start conversations there, so you can always head downstairs.

Each guest has a distinct voice and an ongoing monologue of 20 unique dialogue beats, with at least four complete sentences each. Max goes deep on movies, Jules has snack theories, Bea relives the afterparty, Sam rates every comfort, Leo pitches creative ideas, and Rae has another question before you go. Their dialogue advances every 2.4 seconds, showing one complete thought at a time. Each accepted E, Space, or mobile MASH tap immediately shows the next line and resets its reading timer while still filling the escape meter. The twelfth tap ends the conversation and leaves the next unread line for another encounter. Meet them again and they pick up where they left off, circling back only after their whole ramble. A fresh run resets everyone's stories. Turn Sound on to hear original cartoon gibberish: each person has a distinct pitch, vocal tone and rhythm. The chatter follows each dialogue line, warbles as you mash to escape, and stops immediately when muted, paused, hidden, escaped or restarted. Voices are generated locally with Web Audio; no recordings or downloads are required.

The ground-floor doorway opens into the Snack & Paws Wing: a snack den, Waffles the corgi's clubhouse with the staircase to the upstairs, and a pet kitchen with his food bowl in its far northeast corner. The clubhouse has human snacks around its seating and shelves each run. Nico patrols his den without eating until you first enter that room. After that, he competes for actual wing munchies, with crunchy bites, a voiced Mmm when sound is on, and an overhead thought bubble. His food never adds to your haul or refills your energy. His appetite resets with a fresh run. Call Waffles with E nearby and guide him around the furniture to his bowl. He stops for distractions or waits if you get too far ahead: physically walk back to him and press E nearby to coax him onward. A squeaky toy in the kitchen offers a one-use PLAY interaction while he is following close by, restoring up to 25 of his energy. At the bowl he eats for two seconds, then gets full energy, a wagging tail and happy hearts. Once fed, he follows you through doors and stairs across every area and curls up beside the couch during the ending. His overhead meter begins at 75 and drains 2.4 energy per active second until fed, giving you about 31 seconds from his starting 75 energy. He begs and occasionally whines when sound is on. At zero he curls up and sleeps; E nearby gently wakes him with 25 energy so you can finish the task. The bowl is already stocked and feeding him does not spend collected munchies. This task is optional. Nico and unfed Waffles pause outside Snack & Paws. Fed Waffles travels with you; his hunger no longer drains. The simulation pauses in menus, hidden tabs, and after a run, while the ending shows him resting by the couch. Their state persists between area visits. A fresh run resets everything.

Food is generated from a validated placement pool; the amount and distribution vary each run. Supplies remain hidden until you get close inside the room, or reveal a snack with one of two hints. Discovered snacks stay visible when you revisit their floor for the rest of that run. Room labels and the map show names and exploration status, never snack totals or remaining supplies.

The Midnight Wing is a quieter final section beside the upstairs. Laundry machines and baskets, clothing racks and mirrors, and attic trunks and keepsakes offer optional places to look for snacks. The central hallway leads straight to the private TV snug through its north doorway. Its terrace-side wall is closed, so the moonlit terrace is an optional detour rather than a passage to the couch. You can go straight to the couch without searching or clearing any other room. Guests stay in their own areas, so the snug is a peaceful finish.

Your run starts in the ground-floor Snack Lounge. The final TV snug in the Midnight Wing is marked HOME; the starting lounge no longer banks a haul. Reach the marked couch area with at least one uneaten munchie still in your bag and press E to bank those remaining snacks. Neither entering the room nor collecting all available food automatically ends a run. Your final score is the number of uneaten snacks delivered, so food already eaten never counts. Higher banked counts beat the record, with a faster run breaking a tie. Unbanked food never changes the record. Restart returns you downstairs, restores energy, and regenerates the mansion’s supply. This bag-scoring mode saves its best record under the localStorage key `high-and-seek-best-haul-v2`, separately from earlier modes.


Banking a haul starts a nine-second ending scene before the results: the camera moves into the private TV snug, you sit on its couch and eat the uneaten food you delivered, and the TV switches on to a bundled original Munchie TV cartoon. Portrait screens get separate close-ups of the TV and your character eating before the camera returns to the snug. The local 10-second MP4 loops silently on the TV, so no external video service is needed. P or Pause freezes the scene; Escape or Skip scene goes to the result. The haul is saved immediately, while gameplay time and energy freeze. Hidden tabs pause the scene and video. Close the result with Enjoy the couch & TV to keep watching, or start another run. Restart stops playback and restores the normal camera and controls.
The sound control includes an original 88 BPM soundtrack with separate arrangements for the ground floor, Snack & Paws, upstairs, and the Midnight Wing. Eight synchronized loops—lounge, snack, upstairs, midnight, midnight rhythm, tension, conversation, and couch—each last about 65 seconds (24 bars). Area changes crossfade over two seconds on one shared audio context. Across the moonlit terrace, the Midnight Wing’s musical bed continues while its rhythm thins over 3.2 seconds; the quieter mix carries into the TV snug. The couch arrangement begins only when you bank your haul and start the ending. Pursuing guests add tension, conversations get their own arrangement, and music lowers under guest voices. Sound is enabled by default. Music starts on your first click, tap, or keypress, as required by browser audio policies. Music pauses with menus or hidden tabs and stops on mute or page exit. The sound button mutes all game audio; on phones, use the sound control in the pause menu. The local WAV files are in `dist/assets/music/`; `generate-music.py` regenerates them. Use the local server for music, since browser file-opening restrictions can block audio loading.

The Midnight Wing has a separate noise meter. Walking is quiet, while sprinting, rummaging for snacks, bumping furniture, creaky attic boards and loose terrace bottles build noise. Spinning laundry machines mask nearby sounds; the wardrobe rug softens footsteps, and occasional terrace wind covers noise. A warning starts at 50; at 85, Mara wakes and investigates your last loud position. Furniture blocks her sight, so quietly moving away can lose her. Getting caught starts a fixed six-second lecture: movement stops, E/Space/touch cannot skip it, and time and idle energy drain continue. Pause and hidden tabs freeze the lecture. Afterward, noise drops to 25 and Mara gives you at least eight seconds to get away. The entire TV snug is safe, the resident never enters it, and fed Waffles contributes no noise. Leaving the wing settles the alert; a fresh run resets everything.

`qa-stealth.mjs` verifies whole-wing noise, room masking and surfaces, collision-aware investigations, the safe snug, quiet Waffles, the unskippable lecture, lifecycle cleanup, and the frame cap.
## Verify

Run `npm test`. `verify.mjs` checks all areas, home furniture, spawn, generated supply, guest routes and placement reachability. `qa-pathing.mjs` checks collection routes, explicit home banking, pause/resume, and 160 deterministic random starting positions. `qa-camera.mjs` checks camera transforms across five screen sizes. `qa-energy.mjs` checks energy, discovery, failure and seeded trips to the couch. `qa-haul.mjs` covers partial scoring, return-zone and empty-haul guards, keyboard/touch interactions, best-record storage, and the absence of room count spoilers. `qa-wing.mjs` covers doorway transitions, corgi guiding and feeding, hunger/napping/waking, competing food consumption, pause and restart, and frame caps. `qa-pet-upgrade.mjs` checks the far-corner dinner route, distractions, nearby coaxing, and the one-use toy. `qa-waffles-companion.mjs` checks fed Waffles following across areas and joining the couch ending. `qa-midnight.mjs` checks the new rooms, upstairs doorway, direct hallway route, optional detours, final banking, and restart. `qa-balance.mjs` checks the snack energy budget and journeys to the final couch. Shared walking and area-routing helpers live in `qa-route-helpers.mjs`; it is imported by the checks rather than run separately. `qa-upstairs.mjs` covers stair transitions, floor separation, guest interruptions, pausing, mash input and repeat guards, escape costs, cooldowns, restart and eight seeded upstairs collection-and-banking trips. `qa-guests.mjs` checks pursuit, dodging, sightlines, furniture detours, stair safety and bounded shared navigation. `qa-monologues.mjs` checks all 120 unique lines, timed dialogue, pause, continuation, looping, restart, mash behavior and speech performance. `qa-chatter.mjs` checks distinct voices, finite audio scheduling, sound controls, speech timing and audio cleanup. `qa-munching.mjs` checks finite crunchy bites and voiced Mmm sounds, shared audio, repeated-bite limits and immediate cleanup across game transitions. `qa-music.mjs` checks original loop assets, synchronized playback, scene mixing, pause/mute, async cancellation, shared audio and frame limits. `qa-collision.mjs` checks rounded furniture corners, wall ends, swept movement and sliding. `qa-ending.mjs` checks cinematic timing, pause/skip, actual video handling, frozen scoring, selected snacks, frame caps and restart cleanup. Set `QA_FRAME_MS=1000` for path checks or `QA_SEARCH_SECONDS=8` for energy checks to exercise throttled frames and extra search time.

Mansion artwork is drawn directly in canvas. The TV uses the bundled `dist/assets/lounge-tv.mp4`; `generate-tv-video.py` can regenerate it with Pillow and Windows Media Foundation. Google Fonts is optional; system fallback fonts are provided. Only the best banked haul and its run time are stored, in browser localStorage. The hosting manifest is `.openai/hosting.json` and published static files are in `dist/`.

## GitHub Pages

Play at https://paulalom.github.io/high-and-seek/. The source repository is https://github.com/paulalom/high-and-seek.

The game runs entirely in the browser. GitHub Pages serves the contents of `dist/` from the root of the `gh-pages` branch; there is no server or build step. The `.nojekyll` file tells Pages to serve the bundled files directly. Keep the repository's Pages source set to **Deploy from a branch → gh-pages → /(root)**.

To publish future changes, commit and push the source on `main`, then publish the static folder:

```sh
npm test
git push origin main
git subtree push --prefix dist origin gh-pages
```

Pushing `main` alone updates the source; pushing the `dist` subtree updates the playable site. Sound defaults on and starts with the player's first interaction; scores stay in each browser's local storage.

## Performance

Furniture and interior wall ends use rounded collision outlines. Circular players and guests slide along contact curves using the same swept movement solver, with travel charged by actual distance. Click routes validate each connecting segment against that geometry and reuse the static navigation grid for each area across restarts. Props can set `cornerRadius`; defaults are 0.22 world units for furniture and 0.07 for interior wall ends, bounded by the prop size. Exterior walls contain the player within the estate.

Active play renders at a maximum of 60 FPS, including on higher-refresh displays. The cinematic keeps the 60 FPS cap, and post-result couch/TV viewing uses 30 FPS with a 24 FPS video. The waiting screen uses 30 FPS; paused scenes redraw only when needed, and hidden tabs skip rendering. Movement, hunt time, and energy use elapsed time independently of skipped display callbacks.

Static scenery and furniture use bounded canvas caches, with at most eight million cached pixels. Camera pans reuse overscan, zooming and resizing invalidate the caches, and changing floors releases the previous floor’s textures. Guests and the avatar retain their depth ordering around furniture. HUD values update at most ten times a second and only write to the DOM when their displayed values change. Pickup, retry, and other direct actions update immediately. Pathfinding reuses typed buffers and precomputed grid coordinates. Guests share a navigation field refreshed at most four times a second while pursuing, with cached routes back to patrols; slow frames use short AI steps to preserve pursuit speed.

`qa-performance.mjs` checks the 60 FPS cap against simulated 60/120/144/240 Hz callbacks, frame jitter, pause/visibility handling, movement/energy consistency, and HUD mutation counts. `qa-render-perf.mjs` checks cache reuse, floor invalidation, painter order, fallback rendering, and memory bounds. Caching removes more than 80% of steady-frame canvas calls in the renderer harness. These are workload counts, not a hardware frame-time benchmark.
